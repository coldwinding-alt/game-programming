# Halloween Audio Prompts

These prompts are for future audio generation passes. They are written to preserve the current gameplay timing while pushing the sound palette toward a more cinematic Halloween tone.

## Teleport

Create a short fantasy sports teleport sound for an arcade basketball game, dark violet magic theme, reverse suction into a bright moon-white flash, subtle spectral crackle, elegant not cheesy, clean attack, compact tail, no voices, no UI beep, transparent background audio, suitable for a fast player vanish and reappear skill, around half a second.

## Shield Seal

Create a defensive magic barrier impact sound for an arcade basketball game, heavy occult seal dropping in front of the backboard, low obsidian thump, rim-edge electric arcs, faint ember hiss, premium cinematic texture, readable gameplay timing, no voice, no thunder boom, around half a second.

## Mega Dunk Start

Create a super dunk startup sound for an arcade basketball game, molten pumpkin ember energy building into a powerful launch, low-frequency rise, fiery crackle, controlled impact, athletic and aggressive, dark Halloween palette, not explosive enough to mask later rim sounds, around seven tenths of a second.

## Dash

Create a fast movement dash sound for an arcade basketball game, razor wind sweep with supernatural smoke, tight transient, sleek forward motion, dark silver and violet feeling, no weapon slash, no vocal breath, short arcade-friendly tail, around two tenths of a second.

## Super Dash

Create an upgraded dash skill sound for an arcade basketball game, stronger than a normal dash, layered wind rush, shadowy thrust, thin magical streaks, cinematic but still responsive, no engine noise, no explosion, around half a second.

## Energy Ready

Create a super meter ready cue for an arcade basketball game, dark magical charge complete, quick premium chime with spectral shimmer, clearly positive and readable, no coin sound, no menu jingle feel, compact tail, around half a second.

## Stun

Create a stun hit sound for an arcade basketball game, disorienting magical wobble, eerie metallic ring, compact and readable, playful-dark Halloween tone, no cartoon boing, no electric zap cliché, around four tenths of a second.

## Swoosh

Create a generic action swoosh for an arcade basketball game, used for throw, steal, and dunk motion, sharp air cut with refined body, not a sword slash, not cloth rustle, high-end sports fantasy styling, very short and punchy.

## Hard Contact

Create a hard gameplay contact sound for an arcade basketball game, used for steal, block, and shield deflection, dark metallic hit with magical edge, fast transient, premium texture, no rim clang, no backboard sample, around two tenths of a second.

## Match Cues And UI

Create a pack of short interface and match-state sounds for an arcade basketball game with Halloween presentation: one haunted button tap, one ritual-style countdown tick, one eerie referee whistle, and one dark arena buzzer. Keep each sound highly readable, arcade-fast, polished, and free of voices or crowd ambience.
